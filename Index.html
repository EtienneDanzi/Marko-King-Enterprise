About Danzie

Travel Crafted with Passion

Founded in Accra, Ghana in 2010, Danzie Travel & Tours began with a simple belief: everyone deserves to experience the world without stress. Today we serve thousands of travellers yearly, offering seamless journeys to 60+ destinations across Africa and beyond.

Our team of certified travel consultants brings first-hand knowledge of every destination we recommend — because the best advice comes from those who've lived the experience.
